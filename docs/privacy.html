Car Info — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · App: Car Info (com.mtgofa.carinfo)

In short: Car Info has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Everything it reads from your car stays on your phone. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What the app does

Car Info connects to an ELM327 OBD-II adapter plugged into your car and shows live readings (speed, RPM, temperatures, fuel use and more), reads and clears fault codes, records trips, and can show a dashboard on Android Auto.

Data the app handles

DataWhyWhere it goes
Car readings, fault codes, VINTo show them to you and detect your car modelStays on your phone only
Trip recordsTrip history you start yourselfSaved in the app's private storage. Shared only if you tap Share.
Settings and Android Auto layoutTo remember your choicesStays on your phone only
Bluetooth device name/address of the adapterTo reconnect to it automaticallyStays on your phone only

None of this is sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth) — to find and connect to your OBD-II adapter. The app does not use Bluetooth to work out your location.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only) — those Android versions require it to scan for Bluetooth devices. The app never reads or stores your location.
  • Network access — to talk to Wi-Fi OBD-II adapters on the local network.
  • Notifications and foreground service — to keep recording a trip with the screen off and show a notification with a Stop button.
  • Android Auto — to draw the dashboard on the car screen.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. If you install the app from Google Play, Google's own privacy policy applies to the Play Store itself.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
